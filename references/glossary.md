# Glossary

**Basis** — the amount subtracted from proceeds to compute gain. Unknown basis
is unknown, never zero.

**Covered security** — one whose basis the custodian reports to the IRS.
Noncovered basis needs provenance before it is relied on.

**Coverage interval** — the date range of transaction history actually held for
an account. It determines whether a wash-sale screen is complete.

**Holding period** — begins the day after acquisition. Long-term requires more
than one year, so a sale on the anniversary is short-term.

**Reasonable snapshot** — an immutable freeze of lots and transactions. It makes
analysis reproducible; it does not prove a scenario is still executable.

**Tax unit** — a taxpayer. Gain budgets and wash-sale scope key to this, not to
the household.

**Wash sale** — a loss sale with a replacement purchase of substantially
identical stock within 30 days before or after. A replacement in an IRA
permanently disallows the loss (Rev. Rul. 2008-5).
